George Brown Polytechnic (GBC)
========================================================

About the School:
George Brown is a public polytechnic in downtown Toronto. It opened in
1967 and takes its name from George Brown, a Father of Confederation
and the founder of The Globe newspaper. The school is known for linking
classroom learning directly to industry needs.

My Program:
I am in the Computer Programming and Analysis program T177 after 1 year I switched to Online (T197) in the
School of Computer Technology. The program covers software design,
web and mobile development, databases, cloud and DevOps practices,
and ends with a team capstone project.

Why George Brown:
I wanted a program that puts real projects in front of me early. The
courses are built around labs, group work and tools that companies
actually use, which lets me apply what I learn to my own software
