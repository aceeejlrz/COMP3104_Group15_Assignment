George Brown Polytechnics
-------------------------

[Overview:]
George Brown Polytechnic which was formerly called George Brown College is a public
post-secondary institution located in downtown Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It was
founded in 1967 as part of Ontario's new system of colleges of applied arts and
technology. The institution "George Brown" was named after a 19th-century Scottish-Canadian
journalist, politician and father of Confederation who founded the globe newspaper.

[Campuses:]
George Brown Polytechnic has three main campuses. There is St.James located downtown (200 King Street East), 
Casa Loma located midtown (160 Kendal Avenue), 
and Waterfront located on Toronto's harbour (51 Dockside Drive and 3 Lower Jarvis Street).

[Programs:]
The school offers certificates, diplomas, advance diplomas, bachelor's degrees, graduate certificates and
apprenticeship training. It has a wide variety of studies such as business, computer technology, design,
health sciences, hospitality and culinary arts, construction and engineering technologies, and community services.

[Learning:]
The school mainly focuses on hands-on, and career-oriented education with many programs including co-op placements,
labs, and industry partnerships. This guarantee that graduated students have the practical skills for what
they strived to be.

[My Program:]
Computer Programming and Analysis (T197) as the name implies, covers overall programming and IT analysis. 
We learn to code in multiple languages, cover web and mobile development, databases, Development operations,
and project management. It's a very hands-on and colaborative course with coding tests, and multiple individual
and group projects.
