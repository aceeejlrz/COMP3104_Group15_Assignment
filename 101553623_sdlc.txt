Software Development Lifecycles (SDLC)
--------------------------------------

[Definition:]
Software development lifecycles (SDLC) is the process teams follow to produce software.

[SDLC Stages:]
1. Planning: define goals, scope, requirements and resources
2. Design: decide the architecture, interfaces and technology
3. Development: write the code
4. Testing: find and fix defects
5. Deployment: release the software to users
6. Maintenance: monitor, fix and update it over time

[Importance of DevOps to SDLC:]
Traditionally, development and operations teams worked separately,
which caused miscommunication, slow releases and workflow,
and missed errors and bugs found in later stages.

DevOps matter because it fixes these problems by making the SDLC
collaborative, automated, and continuous which results in faster
delivery, higher quality, and more reliable software
