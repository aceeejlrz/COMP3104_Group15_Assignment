George Brown College
====================

Overview:
George Brown College is a public college of applied arts and technology
located in downtown Toronto, Ontario, Canada. It was founded in 1967 and
is named after George Brown, a Father of Confederation and founder of
The Globe newspaper.

Campuses:
The college has three main campuses: St. James, Casa Loma, and
Waterfront. Each campus focuses on different areas of study, such as
business, technology, construction, health sciences, and hospitality.

Programs:
George Brown offers certificates, diplomas, advanced diplomas,
bachelor's degrees, and graduate certificates. The School of Computer
Technology offers programs in software development, networking, and
game programming.

Why I chose George Brown:
George Brown focuses on hands-on, career-ready learning. Courses include
practical projects, industry partnerships, and co-op/field placement
opportunities that prepare students for the job market.
